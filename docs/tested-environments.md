## Current Boundary

The recorded Claude Scenario 1, 2, and 4 observations do not establish complete Public Core v1 validation on Claude, cross-platform equivalence, or completion of the approved B5 testing scope.

OASYS is intended for LLM-assisted execution generally, but that architectural intent must not be interpreted as empirical proof of equivalent cross-platform behavior.
