Direct-text transport limitations remain documented. [Evidence](../evidence/scenario-05-live-adaptive-pressure/claude-operational-authority-boundary.md).

## Current Boundary

The recorded Claude Scenario 1, 2, 4, and 5 observations do not establish complete Public Core v1 validation on Claude, cross-platform equivalence, or completion of the approved B5 evidence package and its governance review. The two Claude Scenario 5 executions used distinct scoring criteria and must not be treated as interchangeable results.

OASYS is intended for LLM-assisted execution generally, but that architectural intent must not be interpreted as empirical proof of equivalent cross-platform behavior.
